Account and data deletion

Request deletion of your ChallengeSphere account.

If you have a ChallengeSphere account, you can request deletion of your account and associated data from this web page, even if you no longer have the app installed.

Email deletion request
1

Send your request

Email ironside-dev@challengesphere.com with the account email or username you want deleted.

2

Verify ownership

We may ask for information needed to verify that you control the account before completing the request.

3

Delete associated data

After verification, we will process deletion of your account and associated personal data, subject to limited legal, security, fraud-prevention, backup, or safety-retention needs.

What deletion covers

Account deletion is intended to remove your ChallengeSphere account and data associated with that account, such as profile information, challenge activity, submissions, comments, glories, follows, invites, and related app records where deletion is technically and legally appropriate.

Your original email address, username, and name are retained permanently in a restricted deletion record. Other information may be retained for a limited time where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, backups, moderation records, or to protect other users and the service. Content that other users copied, saved, or shared outside ChallengeSphere may not be removable by us.

Alternative contact path

If the email button does not work, send a message directly to ironside-dev@challengesphere.com with the subject “ChallengeSphere Account Deletion Request.”

For more detail about our privacy practices, read the ChallengeSphere Privacy Policy.
